How to read this board
Every cell below opens pre-loaded with a data snapshot captured 8 Oct 2026, ~16:15 UTC — so the board is never blank, even on a phone or an offline file. Press Fetch Live (or open this file over a local/served http address) and each cell tries its agency feed directly; any cell that connects flips to LIVE, and the rest stay on the SNAP capture with a working source link.

Two cells are live-only. Tornado and flood warnings change minute to minute, so they carry no snapshot and show real data only once a live NWS connection succeeds. Billion-dollar disasters and the ≥ $100K tornado damage figure come from NOAA NCEI post-event assessments (published after the fact, not in real time), so those link out. "State-declared" has no single national feed, so it groups federal FEMA declarations by state.
